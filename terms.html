Official Guidelines

Terms of Service

Please read these simple rules before starting a project with Auzent.

1. What We Build

When you hire The Auzent, our pricing covers writing the code and building your custom software. We specialize in custom web development and native Android apps. Our price does not cover outside costs. You will need to pay separately for things like your website name (domain), cloud hosting fees (like AWS or Google Cloud), or your Google Play Store developer account fee.

2. Locking Project Details

Once you log in to your Client Portal and submit the exact features you want, you will have a 48-hour window to make any final changes. After 48 hours, those project details are permanently locked. If you decide you want to add new major features after this point, it will be treated as a new task and billed separately.

3. App Testing & QA Services

If you purchase our Google Play Store 14-Day App Testing service:

  • We provide a dedicated team of 20 real human testers to test your app for 14 continuous days to meet Google's official requirements.
  • While we guarantee our testing work and bug reporting, we do not control Google. We cannot guarantee that Google will ultimately approve your app.
  • Testing will only begin after you have paid the full fee for the testing service.

4. Payments & Milestones

We break down our web development and app projects into simple payment steps (for example: 30% to start, 60% for a beta preview, 100% at launch). You can easily track these steps in your Secure Client Portal. We will only show you the live preview or hand over the final source code when the required payment step is fully cleared.

5. Ownership of Your Code

Once you pay 100% of your project fee, you own the final software and source code completely. However, The Auzent reserves the right to show screenshots or links to the final app or website in our Website or App to showcase our work, unless we sign a strict privacy agreement (NDA) before the project begins.
