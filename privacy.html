Data Protection

Privacy Policy

How Auzent safely collects and protects your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

To work with you on your web development or app project, we need a few basic details. This includes your name, email address, phone number (WhatsApp), and your project notes. For security purposes, our website also records your IP address and device type when you submit a project request or log into your Client Portal.

2. How We Use Your Data

We only use your information to help build and manage your project. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Create and maintain your private Client Portal workspace.
  • Contact you about project updates, questions, and payment milestones.
  • Keep our website safe and prevent spam or fake requests.

3. How We Protect Your Data

Your project details, files, and contact information are safely stored in secure database. Nobody can access your Client Portal without your unique Workspace ID and Security PIN. We promise that we will never sell or share your personal information with other companies for advertising.

4. Cookies

Our website uses a small amount of basic cookies to keep you safely logged in while you use your Client Portal. We do not use harmful ad-tracking cookies to follow you across the internet.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about how we keep your project details safe, you can reach out to us at any time.
